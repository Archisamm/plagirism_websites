What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work without proper attribution. It includes:

  • 📝 Copy-pasting text without quotes
  • 🔄 Paraphrasing without citation
  • 🎨 Using images without permission
  • 💡 Stealing ideas or concepts
